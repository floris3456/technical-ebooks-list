Active entries here do not count toward2,000. Incidental search titles are not automatically evaluated works.

Keep compact edition-aware decisions with stable IDs, evidence and reconsideration conditions. On promotion retain the earlier reason and mark it superseded. Availability, price and age are never rejection grounds.
