# Rejections and corrective decisions

This ledger records rejected **claims, assumptions, or selection rules** separately from rejected **works**. Do not treat an uncounted candidate as a negative verdict. No works have been removed from the repository to date. The former Jorgensen fifth-edition candidate is now recommended on new original edition-specific and independent teaching evidence; R019 preserves the earlier evidence gap historically.
