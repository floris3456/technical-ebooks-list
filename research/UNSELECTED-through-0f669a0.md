# Evaluated but not selected

Retain every evaluated work that does not meet the recommendation criteria, including removals. **Deferred** means evidence is incomplete, not that the book is poor. **Rejected** means a supported quality/scope decision; **retired** records a former recommendation's removal. Active entries here do not count toward 2,000. Incidental search titles are not automatically evaluated works.

Keep compact edition-aware decisions with stable IDs, evidence and reconsideration conditions. On promotion retain the earlier reason and mark it superseded. Availability, price and age are never rejection grounds. The [complete preceding decision list](UNSELECTED-through-f164164.md) is preserved byte-for-byte; current rows update dispositions without erasing their reasons.
