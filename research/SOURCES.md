## Current cycle index

## Rules

Record each exact source URL and outcome in Git immediately after its retrieval batch, before the next batch. Include failed/incomplete attempts; note the actually inspected section/page, source role, document publication/update date when available, and access date. Never turn a snippet into a claimed read or a timestamp into evidence of freshness. Preserve raw source history rather than deleting unsuccessful research.

Each cycle must inspect at least **20 newly explored websites**, not 20 further pages of known sites. Consolidate subdomains and related mirrors conservatively. Revisit known sites as deeply as necessary, with zero new-website credit. State any shortfall honestly; do not open irrelevant pages merely to pad a quota. A high source count cannot substitute for book-specific independent assessment.
