## Protocol

After each external retrieval batch, commit exact attempted/resolved URLs, outcomes, inspected sections/pages, supplied original dates and a separate access date **before the next batch**. Include failed requests, redirects, partial previews and search collisions. A snippet, rating or navigational shell is not a substantive read.

Investigate at least20 new website families and four strong candidates each invocation, without an approval quota. Consolidate related author/provider/institution sites conservatively. Three documents or twenty sites do not automatically qualify a work: their content must collectively support identity/edition, actual contents, independent expert assessment or documented educational use, and present-day value.

Inspect original material wherever accessible and disclose the limits. Possession of a complete copy, format identifiers, price or purchase links are not prerequisites. Keep unknown metadata unknown, retain non-selections in [UNSELECTED](UNSELECTED.md), and record claim corrections in [REJECTIONS](REJECTIONS.md). No purchases or unauthorized-copy storage. Review statuses and final-audit completion are distinct from source counts.
