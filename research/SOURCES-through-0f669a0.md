## Protocol

After each external retrieval batch, commit exact attempted/resolved URLs, outcomes, inspected sections/pages, original document dates where supplied, and the separate access date **before the next batch**. Include failures, redirects, partial previews and irrelevant search collisions. Do not describe a search snippet or navigational shell as a substantive read.

Investigate at least20 new website families each invocation, consolidating related sites conservatively. Return to known sources for depth with no novelty credit. Research at least four strong candidates without requiring four approvals. Independent assessment/adoption, original content and present-day relevance each need appropriately scoped evidence; three documents or twenty sites are not automatic qualification.

Keep source dates distinct from access dates. Preserve work-level non-selections in [UNSELECTED](UNSELECTED.md) and claim-level corrections in [REJECTIONS](REJECTIONS.md). Availability never supplies a rejection reason; unknown metadata is preferable to invention. Do not purchase books or store unauthorized copies.
