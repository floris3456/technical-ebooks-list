## Selection principles

**Present-day usefulness, not newness.** Older books qualify when their central teachings still apply. Explain lasting value, identify dated advice and check currency-sensitive claims with current primary sources. Historical influence alone is insufficient.

**Availability is not a filter.** Price, paywalls, print status, geography, missing purchase/download links and unknown ebook formats must not exclude or downgrade a work. Format and access fields are optional descriptive metadata.

Each recommendation needs at least three inspected substantive documents collectively supporting identity/edition, actual contents, independent expert assessment or educational adoption, and relevance today. Claims link to exact sources and section/page locators. Publication/update dates are separate from access dates. Snippets, ratings, publisher praise and recycled lists alone are insufficient. Disclose partial previews and edition-specific evidence gaps.

Record every external source attempt in Git after its retrieval batch, before the next batch, including failures. Each cycle investigates at least 20 new websites, not repeated pages on one site; returning sources remain valuable but earn no novelty credits. Source breadth is not a substitute for book-specific quality evidence.

Cycles review earlier work and may add, revise, reclassify or remove entries with reasons. New recommendations require later-run review; final completion additionally requires an evidence, relevance, categorization and deduplication audit. Work-level non-selections stay in [UNSELECTED.md](research/UNSELECTED.md), and claim-level corrections in [REJECTIONS.md](research/REJECTIONS.md).

## Record conventions

One canonical record per included edition, one primary subject and substantial additional subjects from [TAXONOMY.md](TAXONOMY.md). All editions and translations share a stable work identifier. Languages, technologies, concepts, difficulty and formats are independent attributes; incidental mentions do not justify extra subjects.

Use [books/BOOK_TEMPLATE.md](books/BOOK_TEMPLATE.md). Unknown metadata is acceptable; invented metadata is not. Update every affected topic index and count when classifications or review statuses change. No repository index generator or automatic review system is installed; each checkpoint describes the validation actually performed.

## Research foundations

The initial subject vocabulary drew on [IEEE SWEBOK](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering), [ACM computing knowledge areas](https://csed.acm.org/knowledge-areas/) and the [CNCF landscape](https://landscape.cncf.io/). These references are not endorsements of this taxonomy or evidence for individual recommendations.
